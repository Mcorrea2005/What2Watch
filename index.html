Behind the recommendations

How the score works: recommendation score = (normalized movie rating × quality weight) + (normalized popularity × popularity weight). Your slider sets the two weights, and they always add up to 100%.

What the numbers mean: ratings are scaled from TMDB's 0–10 scale. Popularity is normalized across the movies evaluated in this search, so it is a relative comparison rather than a universal score.

A note on data: TMDB information changes over time. Vote counts help provide context for ratings, and movies without a verified runtime are excluded when applying your runtime filter.
