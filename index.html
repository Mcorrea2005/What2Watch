Behind the recommendations

How the score works: quality and popularity are weighted by your slider. What2Watch blends an 8% liked-genre preference into that score, then applies the optional 15% mood-fit bonus.

What the numbers mean: ratings are scaled from TMDB's 0–10 scale. Popularity is normalized across the movies evaluated in this search, so it is a relative comparison rather than a universal score.

Mood matching: when you choose a vibe, What2Watch gives a small bonus to movies whose genres fit that mood. The remaining score still reflects your quality-versus-popularity preference.

Learning from you: movies marked Not for me are excluded from future results. Likes favor movies with similar genres. Reactions and watchlist are saved in this browser on this device.

External ratings: IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes scores appear when available from OMDb; missing ratings are left blank rather than estimated.

A note on data: TMDB information changes over time. Vote counts help provide context for ratings, and movies without a verified runtime are excluded when applying your runtime filter. Trailer links are provided by TMDB when available.
